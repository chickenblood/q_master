Privacy Policy for Q Master

Effective Date: January 26, 2026

The Short Version: Q Master does not collect, store, or share any of your personal data. Your information stays exactly where it belongs: on your device.

1. Data Collection

Q Master does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII). We do not require account creation, and we do not track your name, email address, phone number, or device identifiers.

2. Location Services

To help you find nearby workouts, the app requests access to your device’s location.

  • On-Device Processing: Your location data is used strictly for real-time calculations within the app.
  • No Transmission: Your location is never sent over the network to a server, shared with third parties, or stored by the developer.
  • Control: You may grant or revoke location access at any time through your device's system settings.

3. Local Storage

Q Master saves your personal settings and app preferences directly to your device's local storage.

  • This data is not synced to a cloud or transmitted to external servers.
  • We cannot access this data.
  • Deleting the app typically removes all locally stored app data.

4. Third-Party Services

Q Master does not utilize third-party SDKs, analytics engines (such as Firebase or Google Analytics), or advertising networks. The app is a self-contained experience with no hidden tracking mechanisms.

5. Children’s Privacy

Q Master is not directed at children under the age of 13. However, because we collect zero personal data from any user, we are inherently compliant with COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act) and similar global privacy regulations.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if we add new features. Any updates will be posted here with a new effective date. If we ever decide to collect data, we will provide a prominent notice and ask for your explicit consent.
