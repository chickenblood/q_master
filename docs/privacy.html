Privacy Policy for Q Master

Effective Date: January 25, 2026

Our Privacy Commitment: Q Master is built with a "privacy-first" mindset. We do not collect personal identifiers, we do not track your behavior, and we do not sell your data.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Q Master does not require a user account, login, or the submission of personal information such as your name, phone number, or email address.

2. Location Data & Network Transmission

To provide the core functionality of finding a local workout, Q Master requests access to your device’s location coordinates.

  • Network Transmission: When you initiate a search, your approximate location coordinates are sent to the external service f3near.me.
  • Anonymity: This request is strictly anonymous. No personal identifiers (like your name or Device ID) are attached to this request.
  • Retention: We do not store your location history. The data is used solely to fetch nearby workout results in real-time.

3. Local Storage

Q Master saves user-specific settings and preferences (such as app configurations) directly on your device. This data stays on your phone and is never uploaded to our servers.

4. Third-Party Services

Aside from the specific workout search request to f3near.me, Q Master does not use any third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks (such as Firebase, Google Analytics, or AdMob).

5. Children’s Privacy

Q Master is not directed at children under the age of 13. Since the app collects no personally identifiable information from any user, it is inherently compliant with COPPA and international privacy standards.

6. Security

Because we do not maintain a database of users or store personal information on our servers, there is no risk of your personal data being compromised by a data breach on our end. You remain the sole custodian of the data stored on your device.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date."
